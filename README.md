# TNBC Gene Expression & Drug Response Analysis
#Overview
This project explores heterogeneity in triple-negative breast cancer (TNBC) by analyzing gene expression data and drug response profiles. 
The goal is to understand how molecular differences across TNBC samples relate to variability in drug sensitivity.The work was completed during a summer research internship and is organized into 
two connected analyses. The first focuses on gene selection, identifying informative genes that capture variation across TNBC samples. The second examines drug sensitivity, exploring how TNBC samples 
differ in their response to therapeutic compounds.
 
# Gene Selection Analysis
Goal: Identify genes that help explain variation across TNBC samples.
I preprocessed and explored high-dimensional gene expression data, applied feature selection and exploratory techniques, and evaluated gene-level variability to highlight biologically relevant patterns.

# Drug Sensitivity Analysis
Goal: Understand how TNBC samples differ in their response to therapeutic compounds.
This analysis focuses on understanding how TNBC samples differ in their response to therapeutic compounds. I analyzed drug response measurements, integrated gene expression and drug sensitivity data, 
and visualized response patterns across samples.

# Data
All datasets are stored in the datasets/ folder and separated by analysis type:
datasets/gene_selection/ and datasets/drug_sensitivity/
